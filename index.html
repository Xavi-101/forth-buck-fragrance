Our Story

Where luxury meets affordability

Forthbuck Fragrances is a newly established partnership co-owned by Simphiwe Ngcobo and Phelokazi Gxoyiya. The company aims to capture a significant share of the affordable fragrance market by offering a wide variety of high-quality, long-lasting scents in a convenient 20 ml bottle format, priced competitively at R50.

The model focuses on high volume and accessibility, leveraging digital marketing and direct-to-consumer sales channels.
